# Breed trait sources

Pawprint ships a small offline breed lookup used only as a modest onboarding
starting clue. Individual observations and session feedback take priority over
breed averages.

- Cat traits are adapted from the public TheCatAPI breed schema, including its
  `energy_level`, `social_needs`, and temperament fields.
- Dog trait categories follow the open dog-breeds dataset fields for
  `Energy_Level`, `Exercise_Needs`, `Potential_For_Playfulness`, and size.

The app does not diagnose behavior or health from breed and does not require a
network request during onboarding.
